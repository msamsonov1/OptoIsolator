Physical Principles of Radiation-Induced Optoelectronic Degradation

High-voltage battery isolation in deep-space power management units (PMUs) relies on optocouplers to provide galvanic breaks between 400V direct-current rails and low-voltage radiation-hardened FPGA logic boards. In Jovian radiation belts, trapped high-energy electrons (1–20 MeV) and protons induce severe lattice displacement and ionization damage across both optical silicone waveguides and bipolar silicon substrates.

1

Silicone Waveguide Darkening & Transmittance Loss

Total Ionizing Dose (TID) creates color centers within transparent optical silicone dome matrices via radiolytic cross-linking and localized free-radical defect formation, drastically reducing optical transmission $\tau(D_{\gamma})$ at LED emission wavelengths ($\lambda \approx 850\text{ nm}$).

$$\tau(D_{\gamma}) = \tau_0 \cdot \exp\left( - \alpha_0 \left[ 1 + \left( \frac{D_{\gamma}}{D_{\text{crit}}} \right)^{\beta} \right] \cdot x_{\text{path}} \right)$$
Where $\tau_0 \approx 0.94$, $D_{\gamma}$ is cumulative ionizing radiation dose in krad(Si), $\alpha_0$ is baseline attenuation coefficient, and $\beta$ is the polymer radiation sensitivity exponent ($0.65 \le \beta \le 0.88$).
2

Phototransistor Base Shockley-Read-Hall (SRH) Generation

Displacement damage creates mid-gap energy states in the silicon phototransistor collector-base junction, generating thermally and field-assisted leakage carrier pairs ($I_{\text{dark}}$) in the dark state even when the input LED is unpowered.

$$I_{\text{dark}}(D_{\gamma}, T) = q \cdot n_i(T) \cdot \frac{W_{\text{dep}}}{\tau_g} + K_{\text{damage}} \cdot \Phi_{\text{eq}} \cdot A_{\text{junc}} \cdot \exp\left( -\frac{E_g - \Delta E_t}{k_B T} \right)$$
Where $\tau_g$ is generation lifetime, $\Phi_{\text{eq}}$ is 1-MeV equivalent neutron/electron fluence, $A_{\text{junc}}$ is junction area, and $\Delta E_t \approx 0.55\text{ eV}$ represents radiation defect trap depth.
3

Startup Diagnostic Mapping: Duty Cycle ($D$) vs Resistance ($R$)

During self-test mode, the digital PMU sweeps drive duty cycle ($D$) to measure effective channel pull-up resistance ($R$). Radiation degradation reduces the optical Current Transfer Ratio ($\text{CTR}$), causing rightward drift in required drive duty to pull the barrier out of leakage:

$$R(D) = \frac{V_{\text{bus}}}{I_{\text{dark}} + \left[ D \cdot I_F \cdot \text{CTR}_0 \cdot \tau(D_{\gamma}) \cdot \left(1 + \frac{\Delta(1/h_{FE})}{\Phi}\right)^{-1} \right]}$$
When $D > D_{\text{crit}}$ is needed to sustain $R \ge 50\text{ k}\Omega$, the real-time software observer registers critical optical collapse and schedules immediate channel commutation.
4

Radiation Observer & Multi-Channel Commutation Logic

To eliminate single-point mission failure without incurring continuous high-power draw, an autonomous observer Kalman Filter computes optical health metric $\mathcal{H}_c(t)$ and asserts hot failover within 4 microseconds:

$$\mathcal{H}_c(t) = \frac{\partial R}{\partial D}\Bigg|_{\text{nominal}} \cdot \left[ \frac{R_{\text{meas}}(D) - R_{\text{dark}}}{R_{\text{baseline}}(D)} \right] \le \Theta_{\text{trip}} \implies \text{TRIP\_SW}(CH_{\text{CAP}})$$
Redundant isolation channels employ $\text{SiO}_2$ dual-capacitive isolation barriers, which are immune to optical yellowing and dark-current degradation under TID $> 300\text{ krad(Si)}$.
